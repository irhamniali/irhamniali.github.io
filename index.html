01 / Introduction

About me

I work at the intersection of machine learning, information science, and public service, turning complex data into evidence for better decisions.

I am a Senior Data Scientist at the National Library of Indonesia. My work spans predictive modeling, natural language processing, large-scale data analysis, and research on library services. I earned a PhD in Information Science with a Data Science concentration from the University of North Texas as a Fulbright scholar.

My experience includes national library data programs in Indonesia and research with the University of North Texas Library.
